# Crop illustrations

The app now uses six photorealistic AI-generated PNG images, replacing the initial SVG illustrations at the user's request. They identify the supported crop/form, not an official quality grade. Leafy vegetables are a generic mixed category, not a verified species. Maize is dry grain rather than sweetcorn. No image is evidence of source prices or food safety.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool on 17 October 2026. Prompt pattern: one isolated photorealistic food product photograph on white, natural daylight, accurate crop texture, no text, people or basket. Subjects: dried red beans; dry white maize kernels; dry reddish sorghum grain; small pale-tan millet grain; fresh rough-skinned cassava roots with white cut flesh; a loose bunch of mixed African leafy greens. The original SVGs remain as unused source assets.
